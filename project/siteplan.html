Website Planning Document

WDD 131 • Final Project Blueprint

1. Site Name

Selected Name: BrilloExpress Auto Spa

Reason for Selection: The name combines "BrilloExpress" (highlighting fast, spotless vehicle shine) with "Auto Spa" (conveying thorough exterior and interior detailing care). It is memorable, trustworthy, and directly communicates speed and quality to local drivers.

Proposed Domain: brilloexpressautospa.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the BrilloExpress Auto Spa website is to serve as an informative and interactive digital hub for vehicle owners. It showcases detailed car wash and detailing packages (basic wash, interior deep cleaning, ceramic wax protection, and engine wash), displays transparent pricing by vehicle type, shares car care tips, and allows customers to schedule wash appointments online.

3. Scenarios

Questions asked by target audience visitors looking for car wash and detailing services:

  • Scenario 1: "What wash packages do you offer for an SUV, and how much does a full interior and exterior detailing cost?"
  • Scenario 2: "What are your operating hours, where is the car wash located, and how can I book a time slot without waiting in line?"
  • Scenario 3: "Do you use eco-friendly, paint-safe products for ceramic-coated or newly painted vehicles?"

4. Color Schema

The color palette evokes clean water, automotive precision, and high-contrast readability:

Deep Navy (#0a2540)

Usage: Main header background, footer background, primary headings (h1, h2), and primary navigation bars.

Aqua Cyan (#00a8e8)

Usage: Call-to-action buttons, card top borders, active navigation highlights, and logo accents.

Ice Mist (#f4f8fb)

Usage: Main page background to keep sections clean, bright, and visually separated.

Charcoal Slate (#1f2937)

Usage: Body paragraphs, list items, and card descriptions for strong AA+ contrast.

5. Typography

Two complementary Google Fonts are selected for clarity and modern automotive appeal:

Montserrat (Bold 700 / SemiBold 600)

Usage: Applied to all headings (h1, h2, h3), navigation links, and action buttons for a bold, geometric, modern look.

Open Sans (Regular 400 / SemiBold 600) — Sample body text showing comfortable reading for service descriptions, pricing tables, and booking instructions.

Usage: Applied to all body paragraphs, service lists, form labels, and footer text.
